About Us

Too Faced is a makeup brand that believes beauty should always be fun, bold, and full of confidence 💕 We create products that empower you to express yourself freely from a natural everyday glow to a fearless glam look. With beautiful shades, easy-to-use formulas, and playful packaging, makeup becomes more than just a routine it’s a way to have fun and celebrate who you are. We are proud to be cruelty-free forever & always 🐶 because we believe kindness is always a good look. Too Faced is more than makeup it’s about confidence, self-love, and having fun every day✨

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer Suitable For All Skin Tones?

There are 4 shades of Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer, making it suitable for all skin tones.

How Can I Use the Dual Shades in Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer as Bronzer, Blush, and Highlighter to Customize My Glow?

It's easy! You can use each shade individually or swirl your brush in both shades together for a trending "blonzer" (blush + bronzer) look!

What Kind Of Coverage And Finish Does Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer Provide?

Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer is a sheer-to-medium coverage with a luminous finish.

Can The Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer Be Layered With Other Makeup Products

Yes, Sun Bunny Blushing Bronzer is lightweight and can be used over all of your favorite Too Faced products!
